Fig. 1b — Frontispiece

Every plate on the site is a drawing, never a screenshot. Project pages carry their own figure; the exploded switch stands in for the keyboard the whole site is built around.

Plates are generated line art: strokes take the drafting ink, dimension lines take the accent, so they re-tint with the base and the picker. Real engravings (Ramelli, Leonardo) can replace or join them once the image hosts are reachable from the build.
