( MOCK NOTES )

  1. 1Survey sheet. One peak per essay. Left to right is the date written, the region is the theme, and a peak's height (how many contour rings it has) is its reading time. Dashed lines join essays in the same region. Hover or focus a peak for its readout; click to zoom in and open it. The three regions are hand-picked themes that would replace the tag filter, since 8 of 9 essays fall back to "philosophy".
  2. 2Sigils. Generated from the slug, so an essay always gets the same mark. The tick marks inside the ring count its minutes of reading. The same sigil heads the list row, the entry sheet, and could head the essay page.
  3. 5Timeline. Shares the map's x scale, so each tick sits under its peak. Tick length is reading time. The empty stretch from mid-March to early April reads as a gap in the survey rather than missing data.
  4. !Excerpts. They were cleaned by hand for this mock. The manifest's excerpts start with Notion headings ("Core Idea", "Thesis") and [[wikilinks]], so the real page needs the build script to take the first prose paragraph.
