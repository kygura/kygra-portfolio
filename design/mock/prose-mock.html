Software ventures & craft: agentic systems, markets and the open web.

Málaga

Everything built to set people free ends up as something they need freeing from. I build anyway. Everything built to set people free ends up as something they need freeing from. I quit build anyway.

The premise

Five theses, in the order they were arrived at.

i.

Every system that frees people from the last one becomes the next one to escape. Software is no exception. I try to make things that expect to be outgrown, and leave the door open when they are.

ii.

When an institution stops serving the people inside it, I would rather build the road around it than petition for repairs. Exit is a form of speech. Code is a cheap way to say it.

iii.

A good tool hands your attention back. The ones I trust do one thing, show their workings, and never ask to be believed in. The thinking that makes you who you are should stay yours.

iv.

Markets, agents, the open web, a running track before any of them. The fields were surfaces. What stayed the same was the one crossing them.

v.

Winning is exhaust. Aim at it directly and you spoil whatever was producing it. So I aim at the work.

⁂

No final shape. Only the process, and the wonder that started it.

Writings / 15 April 2026 · 1 min · 256 words

Thaumazein

Gk. θαυμάζειν, to wonder. For Plato, where philosophy begins.gloss

For a long while, I had been in pursuit of freedom, of the greatest life I could imagine for myself, redefining at every turn the very terms of victory so that the chase might never end.

I sought the liberation promised by the invisible hand, by that divine and hidden order of the cosmos; I had long resolved never to become a slave to causality, never to be driven into a life hemmed in by restriction and limit, and so I gave myself over instead to the pursuit of arbitrary goals, of endless wealth and success.

I was running after a spectre that promised deliverance.

The hidden motions of the world have led me here, to this moment.

I have seen much and done much, and still it was never enough.

I had forgotten the beauty that permeates everything.

It is time to lay down arms.

It is time to live now.

⁂

There is beauty in the truth: that the ideals of discovery, freedom, and knowledge carry both the individual and the whole of history toward a culminating freedom.

The spirit of man is the spirit of history.

Now I no longer run from the past.

I run toward the future.

§ Appendix

The design system

Manuscript
v0.1 · mock

  1. Everything read is set in the book face. Everything typed (dates, labels, keys, code) is set in the typewriter.
  2. One column of about 64 characters. Notes live in the margin, never in boxes.
  3. Clay is ink for marks only: numerals, the caret, a strike, a hover. Never a fill behind text.
  4. Structure comes from the subject: parts by month, chapters by order written, minutes as folios.
  5. One moving thing per page. The opening types itself; everything else holds still.
